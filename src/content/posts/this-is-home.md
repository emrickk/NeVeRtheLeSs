Home sits high over downtown San Jose, on the corner of the tower, with windows facing east, south, and west. The sun comes in one side and leaves by the other. It is not a loud downtown; most days the biggest thing moving in the apartment is the sun.

![Golden-hour light on a dining chair, shadows falling on the concrete pillar](https://cdn.theneverless.com/2026/07/miro-chair-and-shadow.webp)

We moved away a year ago. Sorting the photos today, I realized how many I had actually taken.

A few corners come up again and again; I can see it now, these are my favorite spots in this home. As for the rest, they are mostly about the sunlight.
