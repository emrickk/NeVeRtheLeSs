# Voice and translation examples

Read when choosing a register, translating a title or subtitle, or weighing a borderline stylistic edit. These examples preserve useful owner-attributed guidance from the former Claude writing guide. Their July dates are the historical attribution recorded there. The current piece and later explicit choices take precedence; examples are evidence of range, not formulas.

### Voice

Concrete material carries the feeling. Facts, objects, physical experience, and dry turns do the work. Interpretation is allowed when the surrounding material earns it. The prose should feel natural rather than polished into symmetry.

Facts are actors, not plaques (owner edits, 2026-07-21): redeploy dates and history inside the scene instead of parking them in an info sentence (1912 statues as 老演员，目送新修的后辈小路). Narrate small decisions as micro-stories with a setup and a payoff (三个不同的黑让我犯了一小会儿难，最后闭着眼睛选的 Sapphire Black … 我看起来也选得没错). Self-deprecating humor is part of the register (车还是被我的恶趣味悄悄感染). The narrator thinks on the page (估计、确实、突然想到): prose that only observes reads like captions; his participates. The rule also cuts (mendocino, 2026-07-21): a bare fact dies even when true. The 1944 wooden bridge survived his edit because it arrived with irony (屈就于木头), a fresh joke (好在加州也并不缺好木头), and a personal crossing (我们刚刚来酒店的路上就是从这座桥上过); the 1909 first lighting and its lens, accurate and plaque-bare, did not. One dated fact doing work beats two.

The voice talks to the reader (mendocino, 2026-07-21): a fourth-wall aside with an emoji is in range (我知道这几张照片出奇的相似，但是来和我一起看海吧🐶), and hedges are warmth, not weakness: he softened a clean zeugma with 似乎, an absolute (nobody builds facing inland) into a desire (没有人想要), and opened a verdict with 说实话. Drafts that read as sealed essays get windows cut into them by his edits; leave a window open yourself. And name the mundane thing: "someone has lined a windowsill with carved whales" romanticized what was simply the gift shop's bookstore, and his edit named the shop. The prosaic name is the honest move and the funnier one.

The current post and his published posts in `src/content/posts/` are the primary references for how he writes, but published posts are evidence of range, not templates: match the current post's register before importing habits from another post. Take style feedback live in the session; fold durable lessons into this file in the open, through his review, never into private memory.

### Titles and subtitles across languages (owner rulings, 2026-07-26)

When the owner sets a title or subtitle in one language, the other language starts from his line: understand what it means and says, then say that natively. Both failure directions got rejected in the same campaign: word-for-word renderings read like Google Translate, and freelance replacements composed from the post body ("which to a saguaro rounds to zero") are not his line either. The post is still read first, but as the key for decoding his line, not as a quarry for a new one.

- Parse the line before rendering it: who is speaking, what the particles do. 来看海岸红杉林 meant "we came to look" (the post's own 我们过来看看), not an invitation to the reader; "Come see for yourself" was a WTF-grade miss.
- The subtitle never restates the title. 我们又来旧金山的天涯海角了 works under "Lands End, 三藩" because the idiom translates the English title into Chinese; rendering it back into English under a title that already says Lands End gave "end of the world" and a blowup. His own earlier subtitle for the same post is the sanctioned degree of echo.
- Wordplay that cannot cross the language stays behind. 图森未来已经没有未来了 disses TuSimple, never Tucson; the EN keeps the sentiment (the company is gone, the saguaros are fine) and drops the pun. A glossed pun ("Tucson's Future has no future") retargeted the joke at the town he loves and was rejected with profanity.
- Marketing formulas are tells: "Come see for yourself", "Come for X, stay for Y", "Forget everything else". His subtitles are a person talking (deadpan parallels, cheeky confessions, skeptic surrenders), never copy.
- When the native rendering will not come, his own post usually holds it: the shipped Venice subtitle is the post's opening beat ("I came prepared to be disappointed") carrying the zh surrender 真的太浪漫了.
- Current title register, settled in the same campaign: mostly "Place, Region" with a halfwidth comma and space in both languages (根特, 比利时 / Venice, Italy), joke titles allowed (复活节岛。哦不，Rapa Nui / Easter Island. Sorry, Rapa Nui).

## Scope matters

Concision removes redundant framing while keeping useful information, colloquial flavor, and jokes. A topic need not become an announced thesis. A travel itinerary follows the traveler's clock; that does not impose a travel structure on a review or a short observation. Do not repeat a sentiment in both the middle and ending just to make the theme explicit.

The owner cut “The lighthouse does it for a living,” retained a warmer thought elsewhere in the piece, and ended with 回家！ plus the photo. When the owner gives the image the final word, preserve it.

A hotel photo-panel request to remove an unsolicited blurb applies to that panel. It does not forbid composing blog prose when requested. Book-cover crop approval does not govern car photography. A coaster-cover choice is not a universal illustration style. Preserve accepted choices and change the part addressed by new feedback.
